## Intended requirements

Counsel should prepare terms that implement the following owner-selected intent,
identify any limits or conflicts, and explain changes needed before approval:

| Topic                  | Requirement / question for counsel                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Parties and scope      | Identify the actual legal recipient (Darren Pratt or a verified legal entity; the Drelwick brand is not assumed to be one). Define contributions, covered repositories and code/documentation/assets, and whether future submissions are covered.                                                             |
| Ownership              | Contributors retain copyright. Use a licence grant, not default copyright assignment. Determine treatment of jointly owned work and any rights that cannot be licensed or waived.                                                                                                                             |
| Copyright grant        | Provide the necessary rights to use, reproduce, modify, prepare derivative works and distribute accepted contributions in source and binary form. Determine scope, duration, territory, sublicensing and transfer/succession terms.                                                                           |
| Commercial licensing   | Explicitly address alternative commercial/proprietary licensing and relicensing of accepted contributions alongside AGPL-3.0-only Community distribution, including Pro interaction. Submission under AGPL or DCO alone must not be relied upon for these additional rights.                                  |
| Patents                | Assess whether an express patent grant is needed, its scope, recipients, sublicensing, termination/retaliation and compatibility with the intended model. Do not assume copyright permission covers patent rights.                                                                                            |
| Authority              | Require appropriate ownership/authority representations and disclosure of restrictions, third-party material and joint ownership. Determine capacity and any age/jurisdiction requirements without collecting unnecessary identity data.                                                                      |
| Employees              | Determine evidence needed for employer-owned work or work subject to employment obligations; do not rely solely on an individual's assertion of personal authorship.                                                                                                                                          |
| Organisations          | Select a corporate CLA or another explicit authorised-contributor mechanism; verify signatory authority, covered contributors, contributions and changes to authorisation. An employee's individual CLA must not be assumed to bind a company.                                                                |
| Third-party work       | Preserve upstream licences/notices and identify material outside the contributor's grant. Define review/rejection and exception handling; no blanket claim to relicense dependencies.                                                                                                                         |
| DCO                    | Define how per-commit DCO 1.1 certification complements the CLA, including forwarded work, multiple authors and corporate submissions. Preserve the established DCO wording; do not substitute a broader relicensing promise.                                                                                 |
| Acceptance             | No obligation for Drelwick to accept, use, maintain or distribute a submission. Distinguish contributor assent to terms from maintainer acceptance of a contribution.                                                                                                                                         |
| Evidence               | Select an enforceable acceptance method and identity/account linkage; retain the exact terms/version/hash, timestamp, covered contribution identifiers and organisational authority evidence. A PR checkbox or bot success alone is not legal approval.                                                       |
| Changes and withdrawal | Determine revocability, termination, survival of grants already made, treatment of previously distributed versions, withdrawal of unaccepted submissions, changes to terms and when renewed assent is required. Do not promise removal from distributed history or automatic cancellation of existing rights. |
| Privacy and custody    | Approve minimum data, notices, access controls, retention/deletion, backups and successor custody. Separate private agreement evidence from public commit/sign-off records; reconcile DCO retention with applicable privacy requirements.                                                                     |
| Other terms            | Review disclaimers, warranties, liability, governing law, dispute handling and any other necessary provisions. No final wording is supplied here.                                                                                                                                                             |

## Approval and implementation evidence

Before this brief can become an operational process, record counsel's review scope,
date, reviewed revision and outstanding qualifications in a suitable private record,
with a non-sensitive approval reference in the decision record. The owner must
approve the final terms and recipient, select the acceptance/custody mechanism,
and verify individual and corporate acceptance and rejection scenarios.

Test missing assent, wrong agreement version, unmatched identities, missing
sign-offs, absent employer authority, third-party material and changed commits.
Each must block acceptance until resolved. Do not configure an acceptance service
or collect signatures against this draft. No third-party CLA has been copied.

Evaluate the [acceptance mechanism options](contributor-copyright-strategy.md#acceptance-mechanism-options-and-implementation-gate)
against the final terms before choosing a tool. Verify retrieval of the original
accepted version and evidence after a PR comment is edited/deleted, an account
changes, a corporate contributor leaves or an agreement is superseded. A status
check must refer to retained evidence and the current contribution scope; it is
not itself an agreement. Record the tested individual and corporate approval and
rejection cases before enabling intake. All such legal review, implementation
and operational verification remain pending.
